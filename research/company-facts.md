## Confirmed Services and Scope

1. **Emergency Water Damage Restoration & Mitigation**: Rapid extraction, moisture detection, structural drying, and stabilization.
2. **Emergency Water Extraction**: Standing water removal using commercial extraction equipment.
3. **Structural Drying & Moisture Monitoring**: Targeted drying with commercial dehumidifiers, air movers, and moisture meters.
4. **Flood & Sewage Cleanup (Category 3 Water Cleanup)**: Containment, hazardous sewage removal, antimicrobial cleaning, and decontamination.
5. **Fire & Smoke Damage Mitigation**: Emergency stabilization, soot cleanup, and odor control.
6. **Mold Mitigation**: Containment, negative air pressure/HEPA filtration, moisture control, and affected-material removal.
7. **Commercial Water Damage Restoration**: Rapid response for businesses, commercial facilities, medical/nursing homes, and educational institutions.
8. **Insurance and Claims Support**: Moisture mapping, photo documentation, and structured job logs to assist owner insurance claims.

*Important Note on Scope Limitation*: The company explicitly distinguishes emergency mitigation and stabilization from permanent rebuild/reconstruction and cosmetic repairs.
